1 Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Royal Shubham Pixel Labs (“we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you access or use the Royal Spend mobile application on Android and iOS devices (the “App”).

By downloading, installing, or using Royal Spend, you acknowledge and agree to the collection and handling of information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue using the application.

2 Nature of the Application (Financial Services Clarification)

In adherence to Google Play Store Financial Services Policies:

  • Personal Budgeting Utility: Royal Spend is strictly a personal finance tracking and travel planning utility designed to help users manually record and analyze their personal expenditures.
  • No Banking / Credit Services: The App does NOT provide loans, credit lines, investment advisory, banking facilities, or money lending services.
  • No Direct Bank Access: The App does NOT connect to or scrape your bank accounts or credit card portals via financial aggregators. All entries are managed directly by you.
  • No Payment Processing: Royal Spend does not process financial payments, monetary transfers, or real-money transactions. Group bill split calculations are mathematical tracking ledgers for personal convenience.

3 Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data strictly necessary to provide expense management, budgeting analytics, and travel itinerary coordination.

A. Personal & Account Information

  • Authentication: When signing in with Email & Password or Google Sign-In, we collect your email address, display name, and unique authentication identifier via Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile Customization: Optional user display name, preferred currency code (e.g., INR ₹, USD $, EUR €), and custom profile avatar.
  • Offline / Local Mode: If used without cloud sign-in, all data remains exclusively on your device's local database.

B. Financial & Expenditure Records

  • Transaction amounts, timestamps, payment modes (Cash, UPI, Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking), expenditure categories, merchant names, tags, and transaction notes.
  • Monthly budget allocations, category caps, and quick-log presets.

C. Travel Planner & Itinerary Data

  • Trip titles, destinations, origin cities, start/end dates, and estimated travel budgets.
  • Daily itineraries: activity titles, departure hubs, scheduled departure/arrival times, estimated activity costs, booking statuses, confirmation notes.
  • Saved places, landmarks, and geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) that you explicitly search, save, or pin to an itinerary.
  • Transit itineraries (flight, train, bus) and hotel/stay check-in/out records.
  • Group expense splits and simplified bilateral settlement calculations.

D. Biometric & Device App Lock Data

Absolute Biometric Privacy Guarantee: Royal Spend uses native device security (Fingerprint, Face ID, Device PIN/Passcode). The App NEVER collects, stores, reads, or transmits your fingerprint vectors, facial geometries, or device passwords. Biometric verification is executed inside your device's hardware Secure Enclave / TEE, and the App only receives a binary success/failure status.

6 Device Permissions & Purpose Justification

  • Photos & Media (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription`): Used exclusively if you choose to select a custom profile avatar photo from your gallery.
  • Biometrics (`USE_BIOMETRIC`, `NSFaceIDUsageDescription`): Used exclusively to unlock the app when App Lock is activated.
  • Network & Internet (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`): Used to synchronize data with Firebase and stream Google Maps tiles.

You can adjust or revoke these permissions at any time via your device's operating system settings.

7 Data Security & Storage

  • Local Sandboxing: Stored locally in a private SQLite database (`royal_spend.db`) inaccessible to other apps.
  • Encryption in Transit: All communications with Firebase use Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS 1.3).
  • Encryption at Rest: Cloud Firestore encrypts all synchronized data with 256-bit AES encryption.
  • Strict Access Rules: Firestore security rules guarantee that each authenticated user can only access their own documents (`/users/{uid}/*`).
  • Zero Data Selling: We never sell, monetize, or lease your financial information to anyone.

8 Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play Store standards, you have full ownership and deletion rights over your data:

  • In-App Clear Data: Reset and purge local database records directly inside the application settings (Settings > Clear All Expenses).
  • Permanent Account & Cloud Data Deletion: You can request permanent erasure of your account, authentication credentials, and all synchronized cloud records at any time by emailing us at royalshubhampixellabs@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Royal Spend Account". All associated data will be permanently deleted from our servers within thirty (30) days.
